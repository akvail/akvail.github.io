 This Palace can be difficult for first time players who are trying to run throught the
palace in a single day. However with a few fusions and a little prep work, it can be
done even on Hard. Before going in make sure you've stocked up on SP reovery
items and a few for HP.

Before Fusing after the tutorial Register the Personas you have in the Compendium.
We resummon old personas often for their skills they could only have through fusion.

If you've sucessfully negoitated and captured a New Persona, you wont have to
repeat that conversation again with that Persona. Select the "Give me your power"
responce, the select Response 2 "There's no need for that" response. Use that to
capture any Personas you need for fusion quickly. It can help save you money and
it's easy.

Guns have gotten a huge buff in P5R, Mainly in that ammo is refilled after every fight.
If the enemy you're fighting isnt week or resistant to gun damage, dump that
ammo early and often to help save on SP. Use it in fights as an easy knock down to
help chain Baton Passes.

Save that lockpick for the Second locked chest in the Palace. It has an a
weapon for Ann thats better to get now, rather than wait.

Savage enemies are week to Ice (Bufu) but its still better to wait until Ann has
Dormina to inflict sleep to deny the shadow its turn during an ambush.

 The first time you enter the Palace will throw two difficult fights at you. Make use of Ann's Dormina, Ryuji's Headbutt or Any other Disable skills to stay in control of the fight

Oni can do alot of damage on hard difficulty using Rampage. Use ailments like Pulinpa or Dormina to prevent Oni from attacking and to help set up technical attacks. Use Lucky Punch to crit and set up a Hold Up.

Keep Ann and Morgana in your party to take advantafe of the new Showtime Attacks. Showtime will target whoever is currently being targeted when you press down on the Touchpad.

The Treasure Demon was common enough that after you can itemize it early and expect to find more later.

When leaving the Palace the twins will stop you to tell you about the new Fusion Alarm. Fusions made during this time will be more powerful than usual, so try and use each Fusion type at least once. Using the same Fusion more that once will likey lead to a random result and an early end to the Alarm.

Also you're not forced to do Fusions here. You can just leave and come back later, or even talk to the Twins to End the Alarm early

During Alarm Time use or summon Arsene and itemize him into a 150 Attack weapon for Joker. Then resummon him after if you're like me and enjoy having him in your stock.
