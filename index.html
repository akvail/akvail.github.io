Alexandria K. Vail

I am a Ph.D. student in Human-Computer Interaction at Carnegie Mellon University.

I was previously a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at North Carolina State Univeristy, co-advised by Dr. Kristy Boyer and Dr. James Lester.

I graduated with B.S. degrees in Computer Science and Mathematics from North Carolina State University in 2015, with minor specializations in Cognitive Science and Physics.

Working under a National Science Foundation Graduate Fellowship, I am currently investigating the variation in behavior between students of differing personalities, self-efficacies, and other incoming characteristics.
